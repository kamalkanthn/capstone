ABOUT THIS DEMO

Honest progress, visible.

This local website supports isolated-sign recognition, recorded vocabulary lookup, online English-to-Indian-language translation, and browser text-to-speech. Translation sends submitted text to MyMemory and may need correction. Sentence-level sign recognition, speech transcription, and generated ISL sign output are not implemented.
